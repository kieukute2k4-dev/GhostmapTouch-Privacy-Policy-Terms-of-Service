2. Summary

GhostMap Touch is designed to process most feature data locally on the user's device.

  • GhostMap Touch does not create a separate developer account for users.
  • GhostMap Touch does not sell user data and does not use advertising SDKs.
  • Installed app selections, macro profiles, marker positions, touch coordinates, and overlay state are stored locally.
  • GhostMap Touch does not send macro profiles, touch sequences, marker coordinates, or the local app list to developer-operated services.
  • Google Play Billing and entitlement verification services may process limited data to verify subscription access.

3. Data Processed Locally

3.1 Installed Apps

GhostMap Touch reads the list of launchable apps installed on the device so you can select an app, attach touch profiles, and start the selected app session. This may include app names, package names, and launcher icons. The local app list is not sent to developer-operated services.

3.2 Touch Profiles and Markers

User-created profiles may include profile names, selected app package names, marker positions, overlay size settings, macro steps, step order, delays, hold modes, and related display options. This data is stored on the device and used to operate the overlay according to your configuration.

3.3 Usage State and Overlay

When you grant Usage Access and Draw Over Other Apps permission, GhostMap Touch uses those permissions to detect when the selected app is active and to display floating controls, markers, profile panels, editor panels, and settings over the selected app.

3.4 Local Touch Playback Service

When you enable Shizuku or an equivalent local system service, GhostMap Touch may play user-configured touch actions on the device display. Touch playback is performed locally on the device in response to user configuration and user activation.

4. Data That May Be Sent for Verification

To protect subscription access and reduce unauthorized builds, GhostMap Touch may send limited verification data to Google Play and GhostMap Touch verification services.

  • Google Play Billing information, such as product ID ghostmaptouch.monthly, purchase state, and purchase token.
  • Play Integrity tokens and related app, device, or license verdicts when verification is required.
  • Technical device or session binding identifiers used to manage entitlement access, including when access moves to another device.
  • Basic server logs, such as request time, connecting IP address, status code, and allow or deny reason.

Macro profiles, marker coordinates, touch sequences, and the local installed app list are not included in purchase verification payloads.

5. Third-Party Services

GhostMap Touch may interact with the following third-party services:

  • Google Play: app distribution, payment processing, subscriptions, purchase restoration, and Play Integrity.
  • Cloudflare or hosting providers: routing and protecting entitlement verification requests.
  • YouTube, Facebook, and TikTok: opening guide, update, or support links when selected by the user. These services apply their own policies.

6. Retention and User Choices

  • You may delete profiles in the app, clear app data in Android settings, or uninstall GhostMap Touch.
  • Exported profile files outside app-private storage remain under your control and responsibility.
  • Verification data and server logs are retained only as needed to operate the service, prevent abuse, provide support, and comply with legal requirements.
  • Subscriptions, cancellations, refunds, and payment history are managed by Google Play under Google's policies.

7. Security

GhostMap Touch limits data sent outside the device to what is needed for entitlement verification. The app may use signer checks, integrity checks, build watermarking, and anti-tamper measures. No system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, but GhostMap Touch is designed to reduce unnecessary transfer of sensitive local configuration data.

8. Children

GhostMap Touch is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided data related to GhostMap Touch, please contact the privacy email above.
